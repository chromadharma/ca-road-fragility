The code in this repository is MIT-licensed (see `LICENSE`). The data it
downloads is not, and keeps its own terms. None of the data is redistributed
here.

- **CAL FIRE** Fire Hazard Severity Zones and FRAP fire perimeters: Creative
  Commons Attribution, per the California Open Data portal. Credit: CAL FIRE /
  Office of the State Fire Marshal; CAL FIRE FRAP.
- **US Census Bureau** TIGER/Line, cartographic boundaries, 2020 PL 94-171, ACS:
  public domain (US federal government works).
- **LA County** Countywide Statistical Areas: LA County Enterprise GIS open
  data. Credit: County of Los Angeles.
- **SNAP roadNet-CA**: no licence stated. Cite Leskovec, Lang, Dasgupta &
  Mahoney (2009), *Internet Mathematics* 6(1).
- **9th DIMACS Challenge** road graphs: no licence stated. Cite the challenge;
  the graphs derive from US Census TIGER/Line.
